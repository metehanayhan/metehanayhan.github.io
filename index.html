About Me

Hello, I'm Metehan Ayhan, a Senior Computer Engineering student.

I am currently working as a Candidate Engineer at Turksat A.S., where I develop full-stack corporate applications using Spring Boot and React. Additionally, I architect and integrate enterprise-level AI solutions utilizing RAG, LangChain4j, and local LLMs.

Previously, I was part of the AI team at Aselsan Konya (Engineer Hub), where I focused on optimizing computer vision models like YOLO and RT-DETR, applying knowledge distillation techniques for edge device deployment.

I also proudly served as the Team Captain for the PSYCHOBOT team at Teknofest, where we successfully fine-tuned the Llama 3.1 model using LoRA/QLoRA techniques on a psychology dataset, publishing our quantized model openly on Hugging Face.

I am passionate about clean code architectures, system observability, and bridging the gap between robust backend systems and cutting-edge artificial intelligence.
